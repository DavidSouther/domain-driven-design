# Books

## Overview

Per-query research against the configured books capability contract published by [`research:configuring-books`](../configuring-books/SKILL.md). The full skill body is written in Step 4 of [`docs/developer/2026-05-11-A-research-books-papers/plan.md`](../../../docs/developer/2026-05-11-A-research-books-papers/plan.md); this stub exists so the skill is discoverable while the per-source reference files are written.
